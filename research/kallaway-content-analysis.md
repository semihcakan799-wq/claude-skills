# Kallaway Content Analysis

Research behind the `video-content-strategist` skill: a study of Kane Kallaway's top-performing public content to extract the frameworks he teaches and the techniques he uses on camera, generalized for any industry.

- **Sources:** YouTube [@kallawaymarketing](https://www.youtube.com/@kallawaymarketing) (about 480K subscribers, 102 long-form uploads); Instagram [@kallawaymarketing](https://www.instagram.com/kallawaymarketing) (education, conversion) and [@kallaway](https://www.instagram.com/kallaway) (tech and AI news, awareness, about 456K followers)
- **Snapshot date:** 2026-10-29
- **Skill output:** `skills/video-content-strategist/` (SKILL.md plus six reference files)

---

## Methodology

1. Listed every YouTube upload with its displayed view count and length, then ranked by views.
2. Pulled transcripts (auto-generated English captions) for 17 top performers: 13 of the top 15 by views plus four framework-heavy videos. Each was read in full and annotated for (a) the framework taught and (b) the techniques used in the video itself (intro structure, ordering, analogies, CTAs, delivery).
3. Pulled indexed Instagram reels with views, likes, comments, and shares: the top 40 of 86 indexed @kallawaymarketing reels and all 17 indexed @kallaway reels (August to October 2026). Captions on both accounts carry the full spoken script, so each was read as a transcript.
4. Flagged paid partnerships (platform partner tags; engagement far below normal relative to views) and excluded them from organic conclusions.
5. Synthesized recurring patterns, removed niche-specific details, and rewrote the frameworks as sector-agnostic procedures with cross-industry examples.

## Key Findings

1. **Craft topics outperform money topics** on YouTube, consistent with his "one standard deviation away from the core pain" principle.
2. **The single biggest video is the simplest:** an 11-minute, six-tip listicle with one end CTA.
3. **Two reusable intro templates** ("Today we're talking about X" and "If you want X, there's one trick, it's called Y") open nearly every top video.
4. **Every top reel names a framework** ("magic box"), and nearly every reel ends with a comment-keyword CTA for a free, topic-aligned resource.
5. **Free-resource keyword CTAs multiply comments** relative to paid-program keywords (median 0.48-0.71 vs 0.19 comments per like).
6. **The tech account's organic winner** follows the full loop stack: new-reveal hook, double-down lock-in, magic box plus head fake, analogy-led mechanism, rehook, stakes, honest twist, shareable last line.
7. **On-camera techniques** recur across videos: meta reveals (the framework used on the viewer), same-story-told-twice, good vs bad examples from his own channel, live demos on boring topics, re-chunking big lists, over-delivery beyond the title, native-embed CTAs.

---

## Limitations

- View counts are displayed, rounded values at the snapshot date; older videos have had more time to accumulate views.
- Instagram data comes from a third-party index (86 of his reels on the education account were indexed); share counts were missing for most October 2026 reels, so engagement comparisons use likes plus comments.
- Transcripts are auto-generated captions; wording in quotes may differ slightly from the spoken audio.
- Correlation, not causation: no controlled experiments were run.
- Some insights reflect his own claims (for example, results from his tests) and are labeled as such in the skill.
